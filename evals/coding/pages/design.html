System design: a URL shortener

Design a service that turns long URLs into short links (like sho.rt/x7Kp2) and redirects visitors to the long URL.

  • 100 million new short links a month; reads are 100x the writes
  • Redirects must take under 50 ms at p99
  • Links never expire unless their owner deletes them
  • Owners see a click count per link, up to a few minutes stale

Walk through the API, the data model, how short codes are made, and how the system scales.
