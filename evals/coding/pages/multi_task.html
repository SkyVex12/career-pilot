Most frequent words

The repository has two files open in the editor: text_utils.py (already written, do not change it) and solution.py.

Implement top_k_words(text, k) in solution.py. Split text into words with tokenize from text_utils.py, and return the k most frequent words, most frequent first. Words with the same count go in alphabetical order. If there are fewer than k distinct words, return them all.

top_k_words("The cat and the hat, and THE bat!", 2)  ->  ["the", "and"]
top_k_words("b a c b a", 5)                         ->  ["a", "b", "c"]
top_k_words("", 3)                                  ->  []
