## 5. Proposed changes

Ranked by how much faster the card shows for the user.

### 1. Prepare an answer for every teammate turn in standups (−0.9 s on 3 of 8)

In standup and meeting modes, hold a fast pass for any "them" utterance of 4+ words, not
only those `likely_question` accepts. The detector still decides whether it shows. Cost:
about 3× more held passes, roughly +$0.05 per live hour on gpt-6-luna. Backend only.

### 2. Decide on the interim text, before the teammate stops (−0.5 to 0.9 s on all)

Start the detector (and the held pass) on Deepgram's interim text once it looks like a
complete sentence, debounced to one call per ~700 ms, while the teammate is still talking.
At the speech end, if the final is not `materially_changed` from that text, the decision is
already there and the held pass is released at once. Today every turn waits for one full
detector call after the speech end. Cost: more detector calls (about +40%, ~$0.02 per live
hour). No precision risk: the same detector still decides. Backend only, a change in
`turn.py`.

(A faster but riskier alternative: in standups, show the held pass at the speech end when
the text has "you"/"your" and a "?" and the user spoke in the last 20 s, and let the
detector withdraw it. Saves the full 0.95 s, but brings back cards that appear and vanish.
Not recommended first.)

### 3. Short deep answers in standups (deep at ~3 s instead of 5.5 s)

For standup mode: deep pass at most ~60 words (3 sentences, no examples), via a per-mode
`deep_pass_max_words`. It shows about 2 s sooner, finishes 4 to 5 s sooner, is something
the user can say, and costs about 60% less ($0.07 a call saved at this rate). The fast cues
stay as they are. Optionally, auto deep only for `technical` and on expand for
`standup_status`/`respond` (10-27 item 5). Backend only.

### 4. STT stalls (−3 to 4 s when they hit)

First measure: log the channel, the time since the last result and what the replay
returned, per stall. Then, depending on what it shows: (a) a make-before-break reconnect
(open the new stream and replay before closing the old one, keep whichever answers first),
or (b) if they are false alarms on non-speech noise (the long "mhmm" segments suggest so),
raise the stall threshold on the "them" channel to 5 s. Backend only.

### 5. No duplicate follow cards (quality)

A teammate line that is only a backchannel ("mhmm", "okay", "yeah", "right") does not start
a new turn for following. Backend only.

### 6. Fast-cue wording (quality)

Fast-pass prompt: never mention notes, sources or what is missing; never ask the other side
the question they just asked; drop a citation without an id. Add Q2 and Q12 paraphrased to
the eval set. When two nodes about one work item disagree, prefer the newest (Q3).

### Recommended order

1 and 2 together take the typical card from ~2.75 s to ~1.2 to 1.5 s; 3 makes the long
answer usable in a standup; 4 starts with logging only. 5 and 6 are small. All are backend
only; no desktop release needed.
